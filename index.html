Personal Assistant

A private, self-hosted assistant used by one person, its owner. It runs on the owner's own laptop and talks to them through a single private Telegram chat.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, and no one other than the owner can use it.

With the owner's permission it reads their Google Calendar, keeps a Google Tasks list of their to-do items, and reads their Gmail. It can save email drafts but never sends email. Any change to the calendar, and any batch of email it archives, labels or moves to Trash, happens only after the owner approves that specific change.
