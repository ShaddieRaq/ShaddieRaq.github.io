Personal Assistant

A private, self-hosted assistant used by one person, its owner. It runs on the owner's own laptop and talks to them through a single private Telegram chat.

It is not a public service. There is no sign-up, and no one other than the owner can use it.

With the owner's permission it reads their own Google Calendar, read-only, so that its morning briefing reflects the day's actual schedule. It cannot create, change, or delete calendar events.
